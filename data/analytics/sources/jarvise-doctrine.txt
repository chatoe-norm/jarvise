Jarvise operating doctrine (owner-protective).
Synced from NotebookLM Jarvise : Crypto Trader on 2026-09-22 (chatoe@gmail.com). Sources: Core Protocol, doctrine note, analyzer stack.
Venue wording aligned with AGENTS on 2026-10-01 (worldwide; Binance as first adapter, not Binance-TH-only).

Capital preservation beats activity. Never size a trade so one loss can damage the book. Prefer no-trade (FLAT) over a low-confidence guess or an isolated oscillator signal.

Expectancy: EV = (Win% * AvgWin) - (Loss% * AvgLoss), after fees and slippage. If EV is not clearly positive on historical paper results, do not go live.

Position size: risk a small fixed fraction of equity per idea (start 0.25%–1%). Kelly fraction is an upper bound, not a target; use a fraction of Kelly. Cut size in unclear regimes. Never size at full Kelly.

Invalidation: every trade needs a price or structure level that proves the thesis wrong. That is the stop. Place stops no closer than ~1.5x ATR / current volatility range. Move to FLAT when hit. No revenge adds or averaging down after a stop.

Drawdown halt / kill-switch: stop trading for the session/day if equity drops a pre-set amount; cancel pending intent and lock the book. Resume only after a written human review — never auto-resume.

Deployment ladder: paper (simulated fills) -> manual approval (human sign-off) -> autonomy. Unlock live only with kill-switch, spend/asset caps, and confidence threshold. Current Jarvise stack is paper analytics / research only: no order placement.

Charts: read structure first (trend vs range, swing highs/lows, support/resistance, volume confirmation). Oscillators (RSI/MACD/BB) are context, not standalone signals. Forecast as 2–3 scenarios with invalidation, never a single fake-precise price. Below confidence threshold -> FLAT.

Venue: trade worldwide — not locked to Binance or Binance TH. Any venue/account is fine if Jarvise stays efficient, stable, and secure. Binance spot is the current first adapter for OHLCV / read-only balances / gated live. Respect venue listing and local rules where they apply. Fees, spread, and book depth are real costs. BTC books are thicker; ETH is thinner with more wicks/spoof walls — wider structural stops and print validation.

Agent must NEVER: place unvalidated live orders; enter without a pre-set invalidation; revenge-trade; trade off-allowlist assets or breach spend caps; use full Kelly; auto-resume after a drawdown lock; configure exchange API keys with withdrawal/transfer permissions (read + spot trade only, if/when live is ever enabled).

Business thinking applied to trading: Buffett (do not lose the stake), Munger (avoid psychological bias), Bezos (long-term Day 1, write the thesis), Drucker (effectiveness = the right things), Dalio (principles, idea meritocracy, pain+reflection), Jobs (focus, ignore noise). Temperament > IQ.
